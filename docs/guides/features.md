# Features guide

## Creating an alarm

1. Tap the **Alarms** tab (bell icon) at the bottom.
2. Tap **Add alarm**.
3. Tap the time to change it. Use the ▲/▼ buttons to set the hour and minute.
4. **Label** — give your alarm a name (e.g. "Wake up", "Medicine").
5. **Repeat** — tap the day chips to make the alarm repeat weekly. Tap again to deselect. If no days are selected, it fires once.
6. **Sound** — tap to select from system alarm sounds or keep the default "Crystal chime".
7. **Vibrate** — toggle vibration on/off.
8. **Volume ramp** — on by default. The alarm starts quiet and gets louder over 15 seconds. Turn off for instant full volume.
9. **Puzzle dismiss** — when on, the alarm shows a math problem (e.g. `12 + 7 = ?`) that you must solve before it stops. Prevents sleepy fumbling.
10. **Snooze duration** — pick 1, 3, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes.
11. Tap **Done** to save.

### Managing alarms

- Toggle the **switch** on any alarm card to enable/disable it without deleting.
- Tap an alarm card to **edit** it.
- Tap the **trash icon** to delete an alarm.

## When an alarm fires

A fullscreen activity appears with:

- The alarm time and label
- **Dismiss** button (large, centred) — stops the alarm
- **Snooze** buttons for 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes
- If **puzzle dismiss** was on: a math problem appears first. Solve it before Dismiss works.

The phone vibrates and plays the alarm sound. The notification is persistent and high-priority — it shows even in Do Not Disturb mode.

## Timer

1. Tap the **Timer** tab.
2. Choose a preset: **Pomodoro** (25 min), **Short break** (5 min) or **Long break** (15 min).
3. Or enter a custom number of seconds and tap **Go**.
4. The circular ring shrinks as time counts down.
5. **Pause** / **Resume** as needed.
6. Tap **+1 min** to add a minute while running.
7. When the timer ends, "⏰ Time's up!" appears.

## Stopwatch

1. Tap the **Stopwatch** tab.
2. Tap **Start** to begin.
3. Tap **Lap** to record a split time (appears in the list below).
4. Tap **Stop** to pause.
5. Tap **Reset** to clear.
6. Laps are listed newest first, with your running time at the top.

## Clock

The **Clock** tab shows an analog clock face drawn on a Canvas:

- Radial gradient background (dark blue or light grey depending on your theme).
- Glass ring with a specular highlight that slowly sweeps around.
- Tick marks for every second, thicker at the hours.
- Large dots at 12, 3, 6, 9.
- Hour, minute and smooth second hands (the second hand has a counterweight tail).
- Digital time overlay at the bottom (shows 12-hour with AM/PM).
- Adapts to dark/light theme automatically.

## Settings

Tap the **gear icon** in the top bar to open settings:

- **Disable battery optimisation** — tap to open system settings and allow Crystal to run in the background reliably.
- **Alarm notification settings** — ensure alarms can show notifications.
- **Grant exact alarm permission** (Android 14+ only) — required for precise alarm timing.
- Coming soon: bedtime/wind-down mode, smart snooze, sleep analytics.

## Theme

Crystal uses **Material You** dynamic colours on Android 12+. Your wallpaper's accent colours are used for the clock hands, ring, and UI elements. On older versions or if dynamic colour is unavailable, custom crystal-themed light and dark palettes are used.
